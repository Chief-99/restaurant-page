OUR STORY

A warm corner, a real kitchen.

Busi's Bakery was born in a Victorian building on a quiet Caoe Town street - the kind with high ceilings, a warm homey feel and the smell of something good always coming from the back.

Every morning begins before sunrise. Sourdoughs are shaped by hand, croissants laminated the slow way (the right way) and nothing leaves the oven unless it's perfect. No shortcuts.

We bake the way your grandmother baked - with patience and love.

- Busi
